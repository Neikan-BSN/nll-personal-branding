Mail Archive Drive Backup — Terms of Use

Mail Archive Drive Backup is a personal-use tool operated by Jeff Browder. It is not a public signup service and is intended only for the owner's authorized backup and recovery workflow.

The owner controls authorization, the designated backup destination, and retention. Existing backups and originals are preserved unless the owner separately directs their removal. Use does not authorize purchases, unrelated file changes, or forwarding.

Availability depends on Google services, network access, the owner's computer, and valid authorization. A successful upload alone is not proof of recoverability; integrity and isolated restore checks are used to validate backups. Keep the recovery key separately accessible because encrypted packages cannot be restored without it.
