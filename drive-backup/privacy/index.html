Mail Archive Drive Backup — Privacy Policy

Mail Archive Drive Backup is operated by Jeff Browder for personal backup and recovery.

Data and purpose. The app accesses Google Drive file and folder metadata, encrypted archive packages, and operational receipts to upload backups, confirm integrity, download backups, and test isolated restores. Receipts contain operational status, counts, timestamps, and verification results.

Permission. The app requests the existing https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive scope. This scope permits access across Google Drive, although the configured workflow operates in the owner's designated backup folder. This app does not request Gmail access.

Storage and protection. Mail archive packages are encrypted locally before upload. Decryption and restore checks occur on the owner's computer. The recovery key is kept separately and is not uploaded by this workflow. Operational health receipts are not encrypted. OAuth credentials are stored privately on the owner's computer, separately from the archive packages.

Use and sharing. Data is used only for the owner's backup, integrity monitoring, and recovery workflow. It is not sold or used for advertising. Public branding pages contain no archived messages, attachments, credentials, or recovery keys.

Retention and control. Existing backups are retained until the owner explicitly removes them. Revoking access stops future authorized Drive operations but does not delete existing backups or local copies. Gmail originals are not changed by this app.
