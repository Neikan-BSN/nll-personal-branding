nll-personal — Terms of Use

nll-personal is a personal-use application operated by Jeff Browder. It is intended for accounts and information the owner is authorized to access and is not offered as a public signup service.

The application provides selected offline email copies and encrypted archive backups. Coverage depends on configured selection rules and successful retrieval, backup, and restore checks. Archives may be incomplete or delayed and are not a full-mailbox backup.

The owner should review backup and restore results before relying on an archive and retain the backup recovery key securely.

The Gmail integration uses read-only access and does not send, forward, modify, or delete Gmail messages. Revoking access prevents future authorized retrieval; previously stored copies remain until separately removed.
